    II.How it works

    The recording is transcribed with word-level timing, and Claude translates it with a glossary, regional vocabulary and a length budget for every phrase. A model from another family then translates each result back to English without seeing the original, so mistakes surface before any audio exists.

    Chatterbox speaks the checked text in a clone of my voice. Every take is transcribed again and scored for speaker similarity, and the best one is kept. For full videos, Demucs preserves the original sound bed and each line is time-fitted to the picture before mastering.

    As little or as much voice data as you have

    Instant

    No training at all. The voice is cloned from the very sentence being translated, so a single recording is enough.

    Tailored

    For the closest match, a personal voice model is trained on about 15 minutes of scripted reading, then applied to every language.

Atomic Resolution Strain Imaging of IFE First-Wall Tungsten with Bragg Coherent Diffraction Imaging

Nels Buhrley · Richard Sandberg · Natalie Coy · Yulin Guo

Computational X-Ray Imaging Group, Department of Physics & Astronomy, Brigham Young University, Provo, Utah

CV GitHub Email LinkedIn

I.Overview

Inertial Fusion Energy (IFE) reactors expose first wall materials like tungsten and steel to high intensity radiation and thermal cycling. Understanding the strain these conditions produce is vital to the construction of future reactors and ultimately power generation facilities.
